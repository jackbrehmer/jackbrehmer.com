Lintel

Real-estate bridge lending fund on the XRP Ledger. XRPL hackathon at Rare Evo. I built the interface and presented to the judges. Tyler Martin built the ledger side.

The fund takes deposits into an XLS-65 vault, underwrites bridge loans off-chain, and originates them on-chain via XLS-66. Manager first-loss capital absorbs defaults before depositors are impaired, and NAV per share updates on every event.

My job was making that legible in five minutes to judges who had already seen forty projects. Most of the work was deciding what to cut. The waterfall and the mark-to-NAV mechanic earned screen time; the token standards did not.
